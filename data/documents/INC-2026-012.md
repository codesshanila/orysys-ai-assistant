# Outage report: payment failures in Fund Transfer Engine

**Date:** 2026-09-12  |  **Severity:** SEV1  |  **Duration:** 128 minutes
**Customer impact:** approximately 4,865 failed or delayed payment transactions.

## Summary
An expired TLS client certificate on the Fund Transfer Engine connection to Visa authorisation network caused customers to see payment failures.

## Timeline
- T+0: Error rate on Fund Transfer Engine rose above 5%; on-call engineer paged automatically.
- T+32 min: Incident bridge opened; payments operations notified.
- T+64 min: Root cause identified.
- T+128 min: Service fully restored and backlog cleared.

## Root cause
The mutual-TLS client certificate used by Fund Transfer Engine to authenticate to Visa authorisation network expired at 07:05. Certificate expiry was tracked in a spreadsheet owned by one engineer who was on leave, and no automated expiry alert existed for this endpoint.

## Corrective and preventive actions
1. Renewed the certificate and restarted the connector pods.
2. Add all partner certificates to the automated expiry monitor (30/14/7-day alerts).
3. Move certificate ownership from an individual to the Platform team rota.

## Lessons learned
Detection worked, but prevention controls for this failure mode were missing or
not enforced. Follow-up actions are tracked in the reliability backlog.
